# Cookie Policy

**Last Updated: July 25, 2026**

## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us provide better service. They contain information about your preferences, login status, and browsing behavior.

## Cookies We Use

### Essential Cookies
Required for the Service to function:
- Session ID (tracks your login session)
- CSRF token (prevents cross-site attacks)
- User preferences (language, timezone)

### Analytics Cookies
Help us understand how you use the Service:
- Page views and time spent
- Features used
- Search queries
- Error tracking

These cookies do not identify you personally.

### Functionality Cookies
Remember your choices:
- Saved filters and searches
- UI preferences (sidebar collapsed, etc.)
- Dashboard layout

### Third-Party Cookies
From service providers:
- Analytics (Google Analytics)
- Performance monitoring
- Error tracking

## Cookie Management

### Browser Settings
You can control cookies through your browser:
- Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
- Firefox: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
- Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
- Edge: Settings → Privacy and services → Cookies

### Disabling Cookies
Some Service features may not work properly if you disable cookies. You can generally disable cookies while using the public site, but essential cookies are required for authenticated access.

## Third-Party Analytics

We use Google Analytics to:
- Understand how visitors use the Service
- Improve user experience
- Identify popular features
- Diagnose technical issues

## Do Not Track

We honor "Do Not Track" signals in your browser. When enabled, we limit analytics cookie placement.
