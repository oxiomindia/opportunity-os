# Compliance

**Last Updated: July 25, 2026**

## Regulatory Compliance

Oxiom Invoice Processing is designed to help organizations meet regulatory and internal control requirements.

## GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)

### Applicability
GDPR applies to organizations processing personal data of EU residents.

### Our Commitment
- Data Processing Agreement (DPA) available for all customers
- Privacy by design principles
- Data subject rights support (access, deletion, portability, etc.)
- Breach notification within 72 hours
- Privacy impact assessments for new features

### Your Responsibilities
- Maintain documentation of processing activities
- Ensure lawful basis for data processing
- Honor data subject requests
- Conduct privacy impact assessments when required
- Report breaches to supervisory authorities

## SOX (Sarbanes-Oxley)

### Section 302: CEO/CFO Certification
Oxiom supports compliance through:
- Complete audit trail of invoice activity
- Exception reporting and monitoring
- User access controls and authentication
- Change management controls

### Section 404: Internal Control Assessment
- Segregation of duties support
- Approval workflow enforcement
- Access logs and monitoring
- Exception tracking and escalation
